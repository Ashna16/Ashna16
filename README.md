## 04 &nbsp; Work at RBC Capital Markets

Senior Business Analyst / Product Lead, 2023 to 2026. Five platforms taken from zero to launch for credit and risk teams. <a href="https://ashna16.github.io/work.html">Full detail on the site.</a>

| | Platform | What it delivered |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | **TMS** | Credit risk monitoring for 1,000 to 2,000 corporate borrowers, US$15B+ in loans. Review time 4 days to 1 day; 90%+ adoption. |
| 02 | **Credit Hub** | One personalized portal for about 45 credit applications, prototype to launch, with role-based access. |
| 03 | **Data Platform Modernization** | About 40 applications migrated to SQL Server, sequenced by a risk model. Zero disruption, zero data loss. |
| 04 | **CCMS** | System of record for 11,000+ borrowers and a US$200B portfolio, feeding 15 apps. Phased migration, zero downstream impact. |
| 05 | **CMAP** | Financial analysis embedded inside the credit review. Weeks to days; errors down 85%. |
